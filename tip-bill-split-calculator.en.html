Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the US, a standard tip is 15-20% of the bill before tax. 15% is typical for average service, 18-20% for great service. If a service charge or gratuity is already included, an extra tip is optional.

Should I calculate the tip before or after tax?

It's best to calculate the tip on the bill amount before tax, not after — since tax isn't part of the service the server is being rewarded for.
